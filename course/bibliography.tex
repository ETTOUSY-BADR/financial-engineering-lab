\chapter*{Bibliographic Guide}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Bibliographic Guide}

The manuscript is self-contained at the level of its main derivations, but mastery
requires working with primary texts and research papers. The following guide names
standard references by subject. Editions differ; consult the edition used and verify
notation.

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Analysis, probability, and stochastic calculus.]
Rudin, \emph{Principles of Mathematical Analysis}; Billingsley,
\emph{Probability and Measure}; Williams, \emph{Probability with Martingales};
Karatzas and Shreve, \emph{Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus}; Shreve,
\emph{Stochastic Calculus for Finance I--II}.

\item[Optimization and numerical methods.]
Boyd and Vandenberghe, \emph{Convex Optimization}; Nocedal and Wright,
\emph{Numerical Optimization}; Glasserman, \emph{Monte Carlo Methods in Financial
Engineering}; Higham, \emph{An Introduction to Financial Option Valuation}.

\item[Econometrics.]
Hayashi, \emph{Econometrics}; Wooldridge, \emph{Econometric Analysis of Cross
Section and Panel Data}; Hamilton, \emph{Time Series Analysis}; Angrist and Pischke,
\emph{Mostly Harmless Econometrics}; Hansen, \emph{Econometrics}.

\item[Macroeconomics and monetary economics.]
Ljungqvist and Sargent, \emph{Recursive Macroeconomic Theory}; Galí,
\emph{Monetary Policy, Inflation, and the Business Cycle}; Cochrane,
\emph{The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level}.

\item[Asset pricing and portfolios.]
Cochrane, \emph{Asset Pricing}; Campbell, \emph{Financial Decisions and Markets};
Back, \emph{Asset Pricing and Portfolio Choice Theory}; Meucci,
\emph{Risk and Asset Allocation}.

\item[Derivatives.]
Hull, \emph{Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives}; Björk,
\emph{Arbitrage Theory in Continuous Time}; Duffie, \emph{Dynamic Asset Pricing
Theory}; Gatheral, \emph{The Volatility Surface}.

\item[Fixed income and credit.]
Tuckman and Serrat, \emph{Fixed Income Securities}; Brigo and Mercurio,
\emph{Interest Rate Models}; Lando, \emph{Credit Risk Modeling}; O'Kane,
\emph{Modelling Single-name and Multi-name Credit Derivatives}.

\item[Risk and market microstructure.]
McNeil, Frey, and Embrechts, \emph{Quantitative Risk Management}; Hasbrouck,
\emph{Empirical Market Microstructure}; Cartea, Jaimungal, and Penalva,
\emph{Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading}.
\end{description}

For empirical data, use current methodology pages from the primary institutions
listed in Chapter~\ref{ch:researchdesign}'s data-source table and archive the exact metadata used
in each project. For any model used in a consequential decision, consult current
regulatory, legal, accounting, and market-convention sources rather than relying on a
textbook alone.
